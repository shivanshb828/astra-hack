Current integration

Live: KiCad inspection and validated placement changes. Cached: improved placement proposal. Not connected yet: live Astra conversation and automatic Blender export. This dashboard polls KiCad every two seconds; it does not mirror its screen.

Placement validation checks the target board, current revision, references, finite coordinates, board-center bounds and allowed rotation. It does not validate electrical connectivity, collisions, thermal behavior or medical safety.
